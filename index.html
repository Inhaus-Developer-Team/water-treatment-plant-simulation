ℹ️ Facility Engineering Overview

High-precision 3D digital twin of a modern municipal Water Resource Recovery Facility (WRRF) with biologically active process simulation.

1. Headworks: 600mm incoming gravity sewer line, motorized fine-bar screen rack and vortex grit settling chamber.
2. Biological Aeration: 4 plug-flow basins with bottom fine-bubble diffusers, aerobic microbial culture and foam simmer.
3. Secondary Clarifiers: Twin 16m center-feed settling basins with rotating scraper bridges and RAS sludge recycling.
4. Tertiary Complex: Dual-media gravity sand filtration beds and enclosed high-intensity UV disinfection lamps.
5. Cascade Outfall: Calibrated Parshall flume outfall tumbling over stone cascade into riparian wetlands at 99.4% purity.
6. Anaerobic Digestion: Dual heated anaerobic digesters, biogas dome, waste gas flare stack and Operations Lab.
