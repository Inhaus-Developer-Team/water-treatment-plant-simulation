Hydro Industries Rapid Deployment Technology
Containerized Modular Process Skid (25 - 50 m³/h capacity)

Engineered for rapid deployment in remote locations, temporary facilities, and critical response situations. These systems combine biological treatment, electrochemical processes, and advanced filtration technologies into a compact, transportable platform with options for diesel, grid, and renewable energy operation.

Pod 1: Electrocoagulation (EC)
In-situ electrolytic destabilization of emulsified oils, colloidal organics, and heavy metals without bulk chemical dosing.
Pod 2: DAF Clarification
Micro-bubble flotation separator floating destabilized flocs to the surface for automatic mechanical skimming.
Pod 3: Multi-Media Filtration
Deep-bed media and granular activated carbon (GAC) filtration columns achieving <1 NTU turbidity.
Pod 4: Automation & SCADA
Centralized PLC automation, power distribution, satellite telemetry, and clean water disinfection injection.
Deployment Applications: Emergency response • Construction infrastructure • Humanitarian & military missions • Remote communities & off-grid industrial wastewater.
